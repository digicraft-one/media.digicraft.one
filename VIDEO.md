1. Curiosity (hook)
2. Recognition (raw clips feel unfinished)

4. Proof (what we do)
5. Trust + CTA (why work with us)

### Brand look

- **Palette:** cool grey, silver, black, white; accent **warm yellow** (`#FACC15` / site yellow) for CTAs and key words only  
- **Type:** Bold display for hooks (Zentry / similar condensed display), clean sans for support (General Sans / Circular)  
- **Motion language:** Smooth ease (power2 / expo), punchy hits on text, no chaotic shake  
- **No UI clutter:** No fake phone mockups unless needed; stay graphic and cinematic  
- **Avoid:** Purple glow UI clichés, heavy 3D chrome, stock “corporate handshake” tropes



### Aspect & delivery

- Master: **1920×1080 (16:9)**  
- Also export: **1080×1920 (9:16)** for Reels/Shorts (same story, tighter crops)  
- Safe margins: keep text inside 10% edges  
- End card hold: 2.5–3s for logo + CTA

## 7. Animation Style Notes (practical)

- **Easing:** Smooth in, crisp out on text; avoid linear  
- **Text:** Prefer scale + opacity + slight y-offset; underline wipe in yellow on key words (`any`, `every`, `designed`)  
- **Camera:** Treat as 3D-ish card (subtle shadow), never distort lens unnaturally  
- **Film strip:** Continuous diagonal travel; sprocket holes can tick per beat  
- **Transitions:** Match-cut through lens glass or film frame borders — not random whip pans  
- **Pacing:** Hook fast; middle informative; ending calm and confident  
- **Loopability:** First and last frames should feel related (same camera world) for About-section scrub/loop use
